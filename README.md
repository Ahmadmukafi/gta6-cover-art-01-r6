An offline web app that turns your own photos into finished GTA VI key-art posters and lets you share them, fully offline in a desktop browser. Entry for the [gta6-cover-art-01 bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).

## What it does

- **Four poster templates** — Boulevard Duo, Pier Nights, Neon Heist and Leonida Noir. Every cover is a multi-panel GTA-style poster: hand-drawn Vice City scene panels, your photo panels, and a bold caption band.
- **Your photos** — load one or two photos from your machine, crop and position each with drag or sliders, fill several panels with one photo or several. Two original sample photos ship under `app/samples/` so you can try it with nothing to upload.
- **Three looks** — Sunset Strip, Neon Night and Noir Vice — plus a **GTA-fy grade** slider (posterize + halftone dots + neon rim light) that drives the preview and the export.
- **Live caption editing** — title and tagline update as you type.
- **Poster view** — one keypress shows the finished cover full-screen.
- **PNG export** — 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080, in one click each.

## How to use

Open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser (or serve `app/` with any static server). Pick a template, load your photo into slot A and/or B, drag it into place, set the look and grade, edit the caption, then export a PNG.

Keyboard: `1`–`4` switch templates, `G` toggles the GTA-fy grade, `E` exports, `P` toggles poster view, `Esc` closes it.

## Offline and original

Everything runs from local files — no network requests, no uploads, no accounts. All artwork is original vector art drawn in code; no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used anywhere.
